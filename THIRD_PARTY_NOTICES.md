# Third-party skills

Khom bundles unmodified skill directories from:

- Addy Osmani, [agent-skills](https://github.com/addyosmani/agent-skills): MIT, copyright 2025 Addy Osmani. See `vendor/addy/LICENSE`.
- Matt Pocock, [skills](https://github.com/mattpocock/skills): MIT, copyright 2026 Matt Pocock. See `vendor/matt/LICENSE`.

Exact upstream commits and SHA-256 checksums for every bundled skill file are recorded in `skill-lock.json`. These third-party files retain their MIT licenses independently of Khom's proprietary licensing. Upstream authors do not endorse Khom.
